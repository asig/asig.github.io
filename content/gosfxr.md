A rewrite of [DrPetter's sfxr](https://www.drpetter.se/project_sfxr.html) sound effect generator in Go and GTK3, written to get my feet wet with UI development in Go.

Apart from GTK3 there are no external dependencies; all resources are statically linked. Built and tested on Linux.
